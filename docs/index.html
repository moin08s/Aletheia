Welcome to Aletheia (Interactive Demo)

What is Aletheia?
Aletheia is the world's first Decentralized Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DeDFIR) platform. It bridges host-level forensic evidence (disk images, EVTX, bash history) with Ethereum Layer 2 rollups and verifiable EAS attestations.

How to Test the Interactive Features:
  • Switch Cases: Use the top dropdown to switch between CASE-2026-LIVE, NIST M57-Jean, and DeFi Circuit Breaker.
  • Attack Scrubber: Click the timeline bars or press Play to watch the breach chronologically step-by-step.
  • Signed .wasp Vault: Click the vault tab on the left to inspect on-chain BaseScan and EASScan records, run live tamper checks, and generate Zero-Knowledge proofs.
  • Court Package: Click "Generate Court Report" in the sidebar to export full cryptographic findings.

⚖️ All algorithms are 100% deterministic (No AI hallucinations), complying with the Daubert Standard & Federal Rule of Evidence 902(14).
